# 05 · The playbook (what to actually do)

Everything in 01-04 reduces to one sentence: **publish original posts that a specific kind of person is predicted to reply to, quote, forward, or follow because of; keep every one clean and legibly on one topic; and get the first like and the first real replies from people inside your lane within the first hours.**

## 1. One lane, 90 days
Retrieval represents your post as author hash + content codes. A scattered account has an author hash that predicts nothing. 80% of originals inside the lane; bio states it; pin the best lane post.

## 2. Design each original for one high-weight action
- **Forward-worthy** (copy link 20, DM 5, share 2): reference posts, decoded configs, checklists, before/after numbers, a tool people can open. What people send to a group chat.
- **Reply-worthy** (5, or 20 from mutuals): a specific claim + a specific question a reader can answer in one line.
- **Quote-worthy** (5): a template or a strong take people attach their own version to. Quotes are originals: they travel.
- **Follow-worthy** (4): visible series and shipped things; a post that promises a next one.
- **Dwell** (0.004/s): dense posts that reward reading; never pad.

## 3. Build the mutual-follow reply graph
+15 reply weight applies only when the viewer follows you *and you follow them back*, on your originals. Follow back every substantive replier; 5-10 real replies a day on lane originals; keep a private list of 20-30 mutuals who actually reply.

## 4. The first three hours, every original
1. Post when your lane's audience is awake (the model sees the viewer's local hour as context, not as a penalty).
2. First like within minutes: it opens the retrieval door for the next 24 h.
3. DM the post to 3-5 people it is genuinely for. Their reply carries it into *their* followers' feeds; their quote is an original for their audience.
4. Reply to every substantive reply, then stop. Your own replies under your post are worth nothing beyond your followers.
5. Don't self-repost or reply-bump.

## 5. Cadence
- ≤ 1,000 followers: 2-3 originals/day, ≥ 4 h apart (author diversity: 2nd post ×0.625), so a fresh < 24 h original with < 1,000 views is always competing for the lifted slot.
- > 1,000: 1-2/day; quality of predicted actions dominates.
- Threads only for follower depth; payload in post 1; sequel as a new original or a quote of your own post.
- Same news as everyone: distinct angle or skip (the DPP keeps ~1 version per pool).

## 6. Keep it clean
See 03-suppression.md checklist. Most "shadowbans" are link reputation, engagement bait, or borderline media.

## 7. Measure what the code rewards
- replies + quotes per 1,000 impressions (rising)
- shares per 1,000 (any post above your median gets a sequel)
- new follows per 1,000
- time-to-first-like (< 10 min)
- first-hour views vs follower count (> 2× = the post left the graph)
- Under the Hood labels monthly (accounts ≥ 1 year old): none
